# MakeRoom demo

One room. Two scattered hours. A customer who needs them together.

The example uses fictional photography studio customers on November 12, 2026, Pacific time. The rate is $60 per hour.

1. Open the studio day. Alex has 2–3 pm, Sam has 3–4 pm. The room is free at 1–2 pm and 4–5 pm. Maya requests 2–4 pm.
2. Open Alex's terms. His message offers 1 pm for $15 back. Sam offers 4 pm for $10 back. If an AI provider is connected, edit a message and select **Read with AI**. Read the source quotation, review the time and amount, then save.
3. Select **Find an arrangement**. Both appointments remain unchanged. The proposed calendar fits Maya at 2–4 pm with $25 in refunds.
4. Open each customer offer. Each link reveals only that customer's proposed time and refund. Accept both.
5. In practice mode, complete the practice booking. In sandbox mode, approve Maya's $145 checkout in PayPal, then confirm the booking in MakeRoom.
6. Check the new calendar and Activity. Alex receives $15 and Sam $10 as partial refunds to their original sandbox captures. The studio adds $120 in gross booking revenue before fees and taxes.

## Sandbox preparation

Activate PayPal sandbox in Connections. Fund Alex's and Sam's $60 original bookings through the Customers screen before creating the arrangement. Use a PayPal sandbox personal account, never a real buyer account. One test buyer can stand in for all three fictional customers during development; that does not represent separate real people.

## Useful challenge cases

- Reduce the flexibility budget to $20: no plan is created; the app explains that the cheapest arrangement requires $25.
- Decline either customer offer: both original appointments stay.
- Change the reviewed terms: earlier offer links become invalid and everyone must approve the replacement.
- Leave a message such as "Maybe later" without a refund amount: the AI must ask for clarification.
- Close checkout: original appointments stay. Use the payment check or cancel controls.

Practice and sandbox results are labeled separately. A successful practice run is not evidence of a PayPal transaction. AI behavior must be demonstrated with a configured provider; the reviewed seed terms are not generated by AI.
